How reliability flows
The same staged controls are applied to every live request.
  1. 01
    Check the request
    Validate scope, length, safety, prompt injection, model, and retrieval depth.
  2. 02
    Retrieve repository evidence
    Embed the question, search the vector index, and collect the most relevant chunks.
  3. 03
    Require sufficient evidence
    Refuse before model generation when the retrieval score or evidence overlap is insufficient.
  4. 04
    Validate the generated answer
    Reject empty, irrelevant, or unsupported factual answers before they reach the user.
